# NHS Compliance Rules

## Data Protection & Privacy

### GDPR & UK Data Protection Act 2018
- All patient data must be processed lawfully, fairly, and transparently
- Explicit consent required for processing sensitive health data
- Data minimization: only collect necessary information
- Right to access, rectification, erasure, and portability

### NHS Data Security and Protection Toolkit
- Annual compliance assessment required
- 10 mandatory data security standards
- Staff training on information governance
- Incident reporting procedures

## Clinical Safety

### DCB0129 & DCB0160
- Clinical risk management for health IT systems
- Hazard identification and risk assessment
- Safety case documentation required
- Clinical safety officer assignment

### Medical Device Regulations (MDR 2017/745)
- Applies if software qualifies as medical device
- CE marking requirements
- Post-market surveillance
- Technical documentation

## Accessibility

### Public Sector Bodies Accessibility Regulations 2018
- WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliance required
- Accessibility statement mandatory
- Regular accessibility audits

## Information Standards

### SNOMED CT
- Standardized clinical terminology
- Required for clinical records

### NHS Number
- Unique patient identifier
- Must be verified and recorded

## Security Standards

### Cyber Essentials Plus
- Minimum cybersecurity baseline
- Annual certification required

### NHS Digital Technology Assessment Criteria (DTAC)
- Clinical safety
- Data protection
- Technical security
- Interoperability
- Usability and accessibility
