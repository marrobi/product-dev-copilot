# FCA-Regulated Financial Services Compliance Rules

## Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) Regulations

### Consumer Duty
- Act in good faith toward customers
- Avoid foreseeable harm
- Enable and support customers to pursue their financial objectives

### Principles for Businesses
1. Integrity
2. Skill, care and diligence
3. Management and control
4. Financial prudence
5. Market conduct
6. Customers' interests
7. Communications with clients
8. Conflicts of interest
9. Customers: relationships of trust
10. Clients' assets
11. Relations with regulators

## Anti-Money Laundering (AML)

### Money Laundering Regulations 2017
- Customer due diligence (CDD)
- Enhanced due diligence for high-risk customers
- Ongoing monitoring
- Suspicious activity reporting (SARs)
- Record keeping (5 years minimum)

### Know Your Customer (KYC)
- Identity verification
- Address verification
- Source of funds/wealth
- PEP screening
- Sanctions screening

## Data Protection

### UK GDPR & Data Protection Act 2018
- Lawful basis for processing
- Data minimization
- Privacy by design
- Right to be forgotten
- Data breach notification (72 hours)

### Payment Services Regulations 2017
- Strong Customer Authentication (SCA)
- Secure communication
- Transaction monitoring

## Prudential Regulation

### Capital Requirements
- Minimum capital ratios
- Liquidity coverage
- Stress testing

### Operational Resilience
- Critical business services identification
- Impact tolerances
- Disaster recovery
- Business continuity planning

## Consumer Protection

### Treating Customers Fairly (TCF)
- Clear, fair communication
- Products fit for purpose
- No undue barriers to change/complaint
- After-sales service

### Vulnerable Customers
- Identification procedures
- Additional support measures
- Staff training requirements
- Extra care in communications

## Record Keeping

### Retention Requirements
- Transaction records: 5 years minimum
- Customer records: 5 years after relationship ends
- Complaints: 3 years
- Audit trail requirements
